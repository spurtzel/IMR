# §7.5 Real-World Case Study: taxi (fig:realworld)

The five queries Q1..Q5 on the 100k-row taxi table (B = 25, queried after every batch, selectivities estimated from the table).

| query | pattern | k | dependent conditions | independent conditions | files | used with |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | `A Z* B Z* C` | 3 | A–B: `B.pu_location_id = A.pu_location_id AND B.do_location_id = A.do_location_id AND B.total_amount > A.total_amount + 15 AND B.real_time BETWEEN A.real_time AND A.real_time + INTERVAL '15' MINUTE`<br>B–C: `C.pu_location_id = B.pu_location_id AND C.do_location_id = B.do_location_id AND C.total_amount > B.total_amount + 15 AND C.real_time BETWEEN B.real_time AND B.real_time + INTERVAL '60' MINUTE` | A: `A.total_amount BETWEEN 12 AND 30`<br>B: `B.total_amount > 0`<br>C: `C.total_amount > 0` | `Q1.sql`, `Q1.json`, `Q1_graph.pdf` | Same-route price surge: an identical OD pair priced >=$15 higher within 15 minutes. |
| Q2 | `B+ Z* C` | 2 | – | B: `B.cbd_congestion_fee > 0 AND B.total_amount <= 80`<br>C: `C.cbd_congestion_fee > 0 AND C.total_amount > 80` | `Q2.sql`, `Q2.json`, `Q2_graph.pdf` | Build-up to a fare spike (Kleene FIRST): a run of CBD trips ending in a >$80 CBD trip (COUNT = CBD build-up before the spike). |
| Q3 | `A Z* B Z* C` | 3 | A–B: `B.total_amount > A.total_amount + 8 AND B.real_time BETWEEN A.real_time AND A.real_time + INTERVAL '20' MINUTE`<br>B–C: `C.total_amount > B.total_amount + 8 AND C.real_time BETWEEN B.real_time AND B.real_time + INTERVAL '20' MINUTE` | A: `A.pu_location_id = 161`<br>B: `B.pu_location_id = 161`<br>C: `C.pu_location_id = 161` | `Q3.sql`, `Q3.json`, `Q3_graph.pdf` | Surge run-up: three Midtown pickups with strictly rising fares, each within 20 min of the last. |
| Q4 | `A Z* B Z* C` | 3 | A–B: `B.pu_location_id = A.do_location_id AND B.real_time BETWEEN A.real_time AND A.real_time + INTERVAL '15' MINUTE`<br>A–C: `C.pu_location_id = A.do_location_id AND C.real_time BETWEEN A.real_time AND A.real_time + INTERVAL '30' MINUTE` | A: `A.total_amount BETWEEN 15 AND 60`<br>B: `B.total_amount > 0`<br>C: `C.total_amount > 0` | `Q4.sql`, `Q4.json`, `Q4_graph.pdf` | Drop-off turnover hub: a trip's drop zone spawns two fresh pickups within 15 and 30 minutes. |
| Q5 | `A Z* B Z* C` | 3 | A–B: `B.pu_location_id = A.do_location_id AND B.do_location_id = A.pu_location_id AND B.real_time BETWEEN A.real_time AND A.real_time + INTERVAL '90' MINUTE`<br>B–C: `C.pu_location_id = B.do_location_id AND C.do_location_id = B.pu_location_id AND C.real_time BETWEEN B.real_time AND B.real_time + INTERVAL '24' HOUR`<br>A–C: `C.real_time BETWEEN A.real_time AND A.real_time + INTERVAL '24' HOUR` | A: `A.pu_location_id = 132`<br>B: `B.total_amount > 0`<br>C: `C.total_amount > 0` | `Q5.sql`, `Q5.json`, `Q5_graph.pdf` | Airport return demand: a JFK pickup to a zone, then a trip from that zone back to JFK within 90 minutes. |
